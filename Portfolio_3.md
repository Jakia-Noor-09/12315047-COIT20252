**Robotic Process Automation for Business Process Efficiency**

Robotic Process Automation uses software robots to do structured rule‑based tasks that people used to do by hand. RPA can cut down processing time boost accuracy and keep consistency. It also lets employees focus on tasks that need judgement or talking to customers. **Selecting and Implementing Suitable Processes for RPA**

Not every business process is a fit for RPA. Processes that are standard repetitive high in volume based on rules and take a lot of time are usually choices. **Process Cybersecurity and the CIA Triad**

Process cybersecurity helps keep business processes and the systems that support them safe, from cyber threats. A main idea is the CIA Triad which includes confidentiality integrity and availability. Confidentiality keeps information from people who are not allowed to see it. Integrity keeps information and process activities from changes that are not allowed while availability makes sure that people who are allowed can use systems when they need to. Cyber incidents can hurt business activities and the IT assets that support them instead of just one single system (Raptaki, Stergiopoulos & Gritzalis 2025 p. 3). Springer

This artefact helped me learn about cybersecurity from a process point of view. Before I thought of cybersecurity as protecting computers and data. Now I know that a broken system can stop connected activities. This shows my learning because I can connect the CIA principles to business continuity. It is proof because BPM professionals need to think about security when they look at designing and automating processes.

**Cybersecurity Risk Management Using NIST CSF 2.0**

The NIST Cybersecurity Framework offers a path for managing cyber risk. CSF 2.0 lists six core functions: Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond and Recover. Govern sets responsibility and direction for risk. Identify looks at assets and risks. Protect puts in place safeguards. Detect spots incidents. Respond handles the incident and Recover brings services back to normal. Adding Govern has made the tie between cybersecurity and organisational management stronger (Zakharova & Partyka 2025 p. 339). ResearchGate

I learned that cybersecurity is not about stopping attacks. Organisations also need to be ready to spot, handle and fix incidents. This idea fits well with the university data‑breach example in Tutorial 8.8. The risk‑assessment steps, in Week 9. This artefact shows that I have understood that cybersecurity is a BPM duty that includes governance controls, monitoring, response and ongoing improvement.
