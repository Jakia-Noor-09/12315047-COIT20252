## Continuous Business Process Improvement

A Classification for Systems of Process Enhancement and Innovation by Fehrer Moder and Röglinger
Ongoing process enhancement involves the regular assessment and refinement of business operations to ensure they consistently align with organizational objectives. It functions by analyzing the existing AS-IS process, identifying its weaknesses, creating enhanced TO-BE alternatives, and then tracking results following the changes. This is important since processes need to adjust to evolving technology and consumer demands. Fehrer, Moder, and Röglinger define improvement as examining current processes, then creating, assessing, and choosing enhanced designs prior to execution and oversight (Fehrer, Moder & Röglinger, 2026, p. 739).
From this artifact, I discovered that BPM is an ongoing process. The document showcases my knowledge as it links process analysis to redesign and continuous monitoring. This serves as significant proof of my BPM knowledge since I can articulate why organizations must consistently enhance processes rather than waiting for a significant breakdown.

## Process Governance and Process Ownership

Process Governance Diagnosing of BPM Governance: A Case Study of Facilitators Barriers and Governance Elements in a Hierarchical Public Institution by Correa Vilela and Peixoto
Process governance represents the framework, which is needed to guide and manage BPM process activities. This means that process governance determines the responsibilities, decision-making process, standards, and performance measurements in order to align the processes with organizational goals. Governance is important for cross-functional processes, where different departments are involved and responsibilities are not clearly defined. The good BPM governance ensures the alignment of organizational objectives, roles and responsibilities as well as accountability and improvement (Correa, Vilela & Peixoto, 2025, pp. 851-852).
This particular artefact taught me that even the best process will work poorly without clearly established ownership. In other words, the paper shows my Week 2 learning through the relationship between governance, roles, responsibilities, and monitoring. This is valuable evidence for my BPM knowledge, because now I understand that effective process improvement cannot happen without people, accountability and responsibility.

## Root Cause Analysis for Business Process Improvement

Improvement of Business Process by Quality Evaluation Framework and Root Cause Analysis of AMDK Company DC Bali by Pramesti Githa and Susila
While Process Improvement addresses the visible symptoms, Root Cause Analysis focuses on the reasons behind the problem. Fault Tree Analysis is a type of RCA which begins with the existing problem and further breaks it down into its causes until the root cause is found. This contributes to process improvement since the solution to the problem addresses the actual cause of the problem. RCA can uncover weaknesses in the system procedural training and control which can lead to process problems (Pramesti, Githa & Susila, 2025, pp. 168, 174-175).
From this artefact, I have learnt that the delay or mistake can be caused by a combination of weaknesses. The paper reflects my learning since it uses Root Cause Analysis as a precursor to process redesign. It is important as evidence of my BPM knowledge since I now know that the improvement must address the verified cause of the problem.
